Marble Song

A marble machine puzzle by Erek Studio

Place seesaws, ramps and other parts on a marble machine, press Play, and get every glass marble into the cup of its color. 500 puzzles in 20 chapters, plus a new Daily Tune every day.

Questions and help

How do I restore Premium? Open Settings on the home screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

A level seems impossible. Every level has been checked by a solver. Tap Hint to place one part correctly; the red trails after a run show where marbles went wrong.
