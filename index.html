Marble Song

A music game by Erek Studio

Slide the drum with one finger and catch the falling marbles: every catch plays the next note of a famous melody. 500 levels in 10 worlds, a Daily Tune, an endless Encore, and the 500 classic marble machine puzzles.

Questions and help

How do I restore Premium? Open Settings on the home screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

A level feels too fast. Every level has been played through by a test bot. Watch the shrinking ring on the floor: it shows where the next marble will land, so move the drum there early. Missing a marble costs a heart; after three, you can try again (or, from level 6 on, continue once). In Settings, Relax lets you replay finished levels without losing hearts.

A Classic puzzle seems impossible. Every Classic puzzle has been checked by a solver. Tap Hint to place one part correctly; the red trails after a run show where marbles went wrong.
