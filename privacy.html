Privacy Policy

Marble Song · Erek Studio · Last updated 12 November 2026

In short: Marble Song has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. To keep the game working and free, it uses Google Firebase (usage statistics, crash reports, remote settings) and Google AdMob (ads). We do not sell your personal information.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The app reports how the game is played so we can make it better: which screens you open, levels started and solved, the number of runs and hints used, and whether you played the Daily Tune. Firebase also records an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, language, and an approximate country derived from your IP address. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, a report with the technical details of the crash (stack trace, device model, operating system and app version, and an installation identifier) is sent so we can fix the problem. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Remote settings (Google Firebase Remote Config)

The app downloads small settings, such as how often ads may appear between levels. This uses the same app-instance identifier and does not include anything about you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows an ad between some levels and offers optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as extra hints. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google processes your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), an approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with the ads, under Google's privacy policy. We see only aggregate ad revenue reports.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form asks for your choice before ads load. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted ads. On iPhone, the system asks whether the app may track you; if you decline, ads are not personalized. You can review these choices at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options (shown where it applies). Marble Song Premium removes ads between levels.

Stored on your device

Your progress, stars, hint count, Daily Tune results and sound setting are saved on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Marble Song Premium is a one-time purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Children

Marble Song is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may ask to access or delete data associated with your app instance. Email us with the request; we will handle it within 30 days. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
